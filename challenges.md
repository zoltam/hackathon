## Connecting the physical and digital worlds at industrial sites

**Company:** Valmet

**Team selection:** Valmet is our assigned challenge. The full partner brief has been received; see [challenge notes](challenge-shortlist.md) and [development plan](PLAN.md).

**IP terms:** Your team owns what you build. Valmet can use it in its business. [Full terms](https://sinceai.app/terms#challenge-partner-licence).

**Description:** Explore how AI can connect the physical and digital worlds in complex industrial sites. Build ideas that help people and intelligent systems understand equipment, navigate environments and support field-service decisions.

## Full brief received

**Industrial Metaverse: Semantic 3D Scene Understanding & Autonomous Field-Service Copilot.**

Build a simulation using the supplied boiler-room Gaussian-splat scene and synthetic workbook: extract and 3D-frame many assets, map their statuses, automatically navigate around obstacles to a faulty device, display nearby asset information, and provide evidence-grounded diagnostics on arrival.

Scoring: **30%** semantic extraction, **25%** navigation/proximity, **20%** diagnostics, **15%** backend implementation, **10%** demo clarity/innovation. The brief favours lightweight visualization and strong backend processing. iOS and fine-tuning are our implementation choices, not requirements.
